## 👨‍💻 About Me  
As a **Data Scientist & ML/AI Enthusiast**, I combine my strong foundation in **Mathematics** with a passion for solving real-world data problems. Having completed my M.Sc. in **Mathematics** from **IIT Madras**, I've built **predictive models** and **automated analytics** at **ERNET India** (IIT Madras Research Park), developed **deep learning models** for **image classification** at **IIT Guwahati** (E&ICT Academy), and contributed to large-scale **multimodal dataset annotation** at **Innodata**.
